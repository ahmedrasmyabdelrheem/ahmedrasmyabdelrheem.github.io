About Me

I am a Data & Business Intelligence Analyst with a degree in Management Information Systems (MIS) and intensive technical training from DEPI and NTI. I specialize in bridging the gap between raw operational data and executive decision-making.

My expertise centers on designing relational Star Schema models, authoring complex DAX measures, building automated end-to-end data pipelines, and developing interactive Power BI dashboards that deliver measurable business impact. This portfolio highlights practical projects spanning customer behavioral analytics, manufacturing operations, and executive sales intelligence.
